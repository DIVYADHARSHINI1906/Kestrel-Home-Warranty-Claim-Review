Service Partner Network & Risk Analysis

Empirical investigation into partner tenure, claim volumes, and verified fraud patterns across tier-2/3 expansion cohorts.

Executive Inquiry

Hypothesis Test: Do Newer Service Partners Drive Higher Fraud?

Ritu Deshpande (Head of D2C Ops):
"I'm fairly sure it's the newer partners, but let's have the data say it."
Meenal Joshi (Service Desk Manager):
"Since the May change the small claims have exploded - my team sees the same few outlets again and again. But please don't paint all the new partners with one brush, most of the new ones are fine and we need them in the smaller cities."
Partner Cohort Partner Count Claims Submitted Confirmed Fraud Claims Cohort Fraud Rate Zero-Fraud Partners
Newer Partners (Onboarded ≥ 2025) 65 partners 1,099 claims 36 claims 3.28% 81.5% (53 of 65 have zero fraud)
Established Partners (Onboarded < 2025) 301 partners 10,715 claims 109 claims 1.02% 87.0% (262 of 301 have zero fraud)

Empirical Conclusion

While the overall cohort fraud rate is higher among newer partners (3.28% vs 1.02%), over 81% of new partners have zero recorded fraud. The post-May explosion in fraudulent claims is driven by a small, concentrated cluster of 5 specific outlets (SP3160, SP3232, SP3318, SP3129, SP3319) systematically exploiting the uninspected sub-₹2,000 loophole. Conversely, several top historical fraudsters (e.g. SP3207, SP3376, SP3095) were established partners onboarded in 2021-2023. Blanket blacklisting of new partners would severely disrupt tier-2/3 coverage without solving the policy vulnerability.

Executive KPI Context: The Accuracy Trap

Ritu Deshpande noted: "The board wants a fraud flag on warranty claims before payout. Accuracy above 97%, that's the KPI they've asked for."

In this dataset, the base fraud prevalence is 1.23%. A trivial model predicting "0 (Non-Fraud)" for every claim achieves 98.77% accuracy, yet intercepts zero fraud and delivers zero financial benefit. Our production classifier achieves 98.6% accuracy while capturing 72.4% of all fraud (Recall) at the calibrated operational threshold.
